# Use Cases
## Recommended Scenarios

### English Copy
#### On-site Card Payment at Public Charging Stations
- Body: Users can pay directly on the payment terminal with bank cards or contactless methods at public charging stations, without downloading an app or registering an account. The solution connects the PTP system with the CPO platform to support charging session management, pricing retrieval, and payment processing.

#### AFIR-Compliant Charging Payment Scenario
- Body: In the European market, public charging infrastructure must support ad-hoc electronic payments and transparent pricing display. This solution helps PTP platforms quickly support direct card payments and meet AFIR compliance requirements.

#### Payment Service Providers Expanding into EV Charging
- Body: Payment service providers can extend into charging scenarios through this platform, enabling their POS or payment terminals to support EV charging and unlock new customer segments.

### 中文文案
#### 公共充电站现场银行卡支付
- 正文：在公共充电站，用户可以直接在支付终端刷银行卡或使用非接触式支付完成充电支付，无需下载 App 或注册账户。PTP 系统可通过该方案与 CPO 平台对接，实现充电会话管理、价格获取以及支付交易处理。

#### AFIR 合规充电支付
- 正文：在欧洲市场，公共充电基础设施需要支持即时电子支付和价格透明展示。该解决方案帮助 PTP 平台快速支持银行卡直付能力，满足 AFIR 对公共充电站的合规要求。

#### 支付服务商拓展充电业务
- 正文：支付服务商可以通过该平台接入充电支付能力，使其 POS 或支付终端能够支持 EV 充电场景，从而拓展新的业务场景和客户群体。
