# Architecture
## Our Business Architecture

### English Copy
#### TrustGuard
- Prefix: Power By
- Body: A PCI MPoC-certified SDK with multiple built-in security mechanisms, supporting contactless EMV transactions and PIN entry on smart devices.

#### Trustlytica
- Prefix: Power By
- Body: A PCI MPoC-certified attestation and monitoring system that enforces pre-defined security policies, verifies attestation data uploaded by the SDK in real time, and issues alerts for potential threats and attacks.

#### KlondPay
- Prefix: Power By
- Body: A PCI DSS-certified payment gateway offering flexible transaction routing, payment aggregation, and merchant/terminal management, effectively simplifying integration and accelerating service deployment.

#### White-label Payment App
- Body: Customizable applications that simplify SoftPOS and QR payment acceptance setup. For customers with existing industry applications, the App-to-App integration mode is also available.

### 中文文案
#### TrustGuard
- 前缀：Power By
- 正文：PCI MPoC 认证的 SDK，内置多重安全机制，支持在智能设备上处理非接 EMV 交易及 PIN 密码输入。

#### Trustlytica
- 前缀：Power By
- 正文：PCI MPoC 认证的认证与监控系统，可执行预设安全策略，实时核查 SDK 上传的安全认证数据，并对潜在威胁与攻击及时发出警报。

#### KlondPay
- 前缀：Power By
- 正文：PCI DSS 认证的支付网关，具备灵活的交易路由、支付聚合能力及商户与终端管理功能，有效简化集成流程，加速服务部署。

#### 白标支付应用
- 正文：高度可定制的支付应用，轻松上线 SoftPOS 与二维码支付受理功能。对于已拥有行业应用的客户，亦支持 App-to-App 的集成模式。
