# Key Features
## Core Capabilities

### English Copy
#### Automated Promotions
- Label: Promotion
- Body: Drag-and-drop to schedule promotional broadcasts by device and time, fully automated.

#### Proactive Alerts for Critical Events
- Label: Alarm
- Body: Automatically delivers key anomalies, such as geofence breaches, license expiry, certificate expiry, and device broadcast delays, to in-app messages and email, enabling rapid operational response.

#### One-Click Firmware Update
- Label: Update
- Body: Remotely update firmware and parameters across all stores in minutes, eliminating the need for on-site visits.

#### Integrated Broadcast & Receipt
- Label: Linkage
- Body: Merchants can enable digital receipts, so customers hear the broadcast and view the receipt instantly for a complete payment experience.

- 标签：营销推广

- 正文：围栏越界、许可证到期、证书到期、设备播报异常等关键问题可自动推送到站内消息和邮件，帮助团队快速响应。

#### 一键固件升级
